ABOUT THE GAME

Jigsaw Solitaire Art Puzzle was built to help you relax while keeping your brain engaged.

Every puzzle features beautiful artwork, and honestly, the gameplay just works. You don't need to rush - just take your time and enjoy it while enjoying the artwork.

There's a timer, but it's not stressful - just helps you stay focused. If you need more time, helpful hints allow you to freeze the timer so you can think, relax, and continue solving at your own pace.

So yeah, you get a puzzle game that chills you out but still keeps your brain working.
